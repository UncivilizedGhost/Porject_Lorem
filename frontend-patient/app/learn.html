A bronchoscopy is a test where a doctor looks inside your airways with a thin, bendy tube with a small camera. The tube goes in through your nose or mouth.

Your throat is numbed first, and you can get medicine to help you relax. Most people go home the same day.

Source Syöpätalo (Cancer Hub), Terveyskylä.fi
