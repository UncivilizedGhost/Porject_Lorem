What would you like to know?

Ask about a test, a treatment or what happens next. Answers come from reviewed patient information — never a guess.

Where the answers come from

Learn rephrases reviewed Finnish patient information in plain language and always shows the source. If no reviewed passage matches, it says so and asks you to contact your nurse. It never diagnoses you or decides your care.

Sources: Syöpätalo (Cancer Hub), Terveyskylä.fi; HUS patient guidance; Cancer Society of Finland.
